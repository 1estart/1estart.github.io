# 🌲 Odintsovo: Is it worth going for "suburban ecology"?

Today, my life partner and I went on a mini-trip – we chose Odintsovo. The city is very close, just 5–6 stops from Molodyozhnaya metro station.

## 🚆 Logistics and Transport

The trip cost 102 rubles. The return journey was comfortably surprising: we got to ride a double-decker Aeroexpress train.

## 🏙 First Impressions

Looking at the map, we planned to visit the large Lazutina Park. It's several kilometers on foot from the station, so we decided to explore the route.

- **Parks:** Along the way, we wanted to check out the Central Park, but it turned out to be under renovation.
- **Ecology:** Personally, I wanted to see lots of greenery and feel that famous "suburban ecology." It's fine, but I didn't see anything significantly better than in any Moscow district or even in Lyubertsy. (Although I've never been to Zheleznodorozhny, so the comparison is conditional 😄).
- **Infrastructure:** The abundance of shops caught my eye. We counted at least five shopping centers and also noticed a four-star hotel.
- **Details:** Right at the beginning of the route, near the church, we spotted a colorful shop selling esoteric and philosophical goods.

## 🌲 Lazutina Park

By the time we reached the park, we already wanted to take the bus back – our legs were killing us. Unfortunately, we didn't go very deep into the park.

- **Atmosphere:** Abundant snow hadn't melted yet in the park, so we could still feel the last piece of winter.
- **People:** There were quite a few people, especially at the rope parks, near the already non-operational ice rink, at the sports grounds, and in the cafes.
- **Observation:** By the way, the bench press machine was exactly the same as the one in our neighborhood – useful stuff, nice to see familiar equipment.

## ✅ The Verdict

It's a good city, like many in our country. We didn't see anything revolutionary, but we had a nice walk. Sometimes just a change of location is already a little journey.
